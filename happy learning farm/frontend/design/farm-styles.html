🌻 开心学习农场 · Style Board

三种可爱农场风格，同一套组件对比。挑一个喜欢的，再把它的颜色变量搬进 common/style.css。

Where to find more cute farm art (free to use): Kenney.nl (CC0 UI packs and 3D farm models), Quaternius (CC0 low-poly farm and animals), Poly Pizza (free low-poly models), and itch.io searches for “cozy farm UI” or “farm GUI pack”. Check each pack's licence before using it.
